How this is calculated
  • Cab booking buffer: 15 min — book before you want to leave so the cab is waiting, not you.
  • Travel time: estimated for HSR → BLR via ORR → Hebbal → NH44 using a 30-minute-slot traffic model calibrated to typical Bengaluru weekday patterns. Crucially, the estimate uses the hour you'll actually be on the road — not when you opened the app — so an evening flight correctly gets peak-hour traffic even if you're planning in the morning.
  • Security: 30 min covers check-in kiosk + security queue at BLR T1/T2.
  • Gate buffer: gates close 30 min before departure; the app targets arrival 20 min before gate close → 50 min before flight total.

Travel time is a heuristic based on time-of-day. For live traffic check Google Maps directions and adjust if it deviates by > 15 min.
