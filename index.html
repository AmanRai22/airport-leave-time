How this is calculated
  • Cab booking buffer: book ahead so the cab is waiting, not you. Bengaluru: 15 min normal / 5 min tight. Nagpur: 10 min normal / 3 min tight.
  • Travel time: estimated using a 30-minute-slot traffic model calibrated per city (Bengaluru: HSR → BLR via ORR/Hebbal/NH44; Nagpur: Midnight Inn → NAG via Wardha Road). Crucially, the estimate uses the hour you'll actually be on the road — not when you opened the app — so an evening flight correctly gets peak-hour traffic even if you're planning in the morning.
  • Security: Bengaluru 30 min normal / 20 min tight (T1/T2 queues). Nagpur 12 min normal / 7 min tight (much smaller airport).
  • Gate buffer: gates close 30 min before departure; the app targets arrival 20 min before gate close → 50 min before flight total.

Travel time is a heuristic based on time-of-day. For live traffic check Google Maps directions and adjust if it deviates by > 15 min.
